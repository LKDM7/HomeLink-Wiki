HomeLink Furnace 0.1.0

A parallel-smelting industrial furnace powered exclusively by HomeLink Energy: no fuel slot, no conversion. Heating, production and keeping warm consume HE from an internal buffer. A power cut pauses progress exactly and resumes it when energy returns.

Principle#

The industrial furnace runs several cooking recipes (smelting, cooking, drying) in parallel on a single block. Each tier raises the number of simultaneous jobs, the number of input slots, the processing speed and the energy buffer capacity. The server is authoritative over progress; the XP from finished recipes is collected by hand (HomeCore CONTROL permission): automation never takes the XP.

Energy#

The furnace stores only HE (HomeLink Energy): no fuel slot. A recipe's duration changes its energy cost, not the tier's speed. When stopped, the buffer recharges and the outputs stay accessible; a finished result waits if the output is full. Link the furnace to an Energy source (panels, turbines, hydro, batteries) via copper cable.

Recipes#

Every tier is crafted at the vanilla table. Each higher tier reuses the furnace from the previous tier.

Block icons: 3D render of their model, as in the inventory. Ingredients: real textures from the mod and Minecraft.

Faces and automation#

FaceRole
TopInput for items to process.
BackOutput of results.
RightEnergy input (HE).

The input/output faces allow automation with hoppers or pipes. A redstone lock (Ignore / Signal required / No signal required) and an on/off button drive the heating. The furnace exposes itself as a HomeCore device: it appears in the Dashboard and binds to a HomeNetwork with the connector.
