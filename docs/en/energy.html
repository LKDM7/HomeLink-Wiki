HomeLink Energy 0.5.0

Renewable energy production measured in HE (HomeLink Energy): solar panels, wind turbines, a hydro branch, batteries and copper cables, all tied to the shared energy network and exposed through the public HomeCore API.

They produce day and night. Wind evolves gradually per dimension; rain multiplies output by 1.25, a storm by 1.60, hub altitude adds a bonus, capped at 150%. The nominal assumes 100% wind, clear sky and a hub between Y80 and Y119.

The hydro branch produces continuously as long as it receives a water flow: the pumps feed a 2×2×2 turbine through pipes. It is the most stable base source, independent of weather and the day/night cycle.

Cables#

Thin tracks placed like redstone, on floor, wall or ceiling, needing a support (solid face or HE port). The network is recomputed on every structural change, capped at 1,024 nodes.

→
Copper Cable×8

Integration#

The Farm, Quarry, Storage and Dashboard machines consume HE through HomeCore's energy contract (EnergyApi.BLOCK). All four declare HomeLink Energy as a required dependency in their neoforge.mods.toml: Farm, Quarry and Storage require [0.5.0,1.0.0), Dashboard [0.1.0,1.0.0). Without HomeLink Energy, none of the four will load.

Install#

Copy homelink_energy-0.5.0.jar and homecore-1.14.0.jar into mods/, on both client and server. HomeCore is required and is not bundled in the Energy JAR.
