HomeLink Farm 1.5.0

The farming module of the HomeLink ecosystem: monitor, diagnose, irrigate, optimize and connect a farm. No resource is ever created; only the FarmBot harvests and replants, actually walking to the crops.

Monitoring and diagnostics#

Maturity normalized 0→100%. Problems are reported only when Minecraft provides a reliable signal: DRY_FARMLAND, LOW_LIGHT, NOT_IRRIGATED, IRRIGATION_OFFLINE. The LOCATE function sends a temporary marker to the relevant player, without changing any block.

Irrigation#

Deterministic rule: one pump feeds at most 5 sprinklers. Beyond that, the whole network goes OVER_CAPACITY and stops entirely. Each sprinkler covers a 5×5 square and grants a +20% growth bonus to the compatible crops it covers.

FarmBot#

The robot never scans the farm itself: the Crop Monitor supplies it with the mature positions (512 at most). It walks there by pathfinding, harvests, replants with part of the harvest, then returns to its station at 20% battery or when its 9 slots are full.

Visible states: DOCKED, IDLE, SEARCHING, MOVING, HARVESTING, RETURNING, UNLOADING, CHARGING, PAUSED, STORAGE FULL, OUTPUT BLOCKED, LOW BATTERY, OUT OF POWER, STUCK, ERROR.

Irrigation view (key I)#

Visual cues above crops: turquoise (irrigated area), coral (network down), gray (unpowered sprinkler), golden crosses (unirrigated crops).
